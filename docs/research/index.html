Research

Questions before titles.

My research is in empirical asset pricing, with a focus on how information, uncertainty, and macro-financial conditions shape expected returns and predictability across equity and foreign-exchange markets. My empirical toolkit includes factor-mimicking portfolios, generalized method of moments (GMM), machine learning, and time-series predictive methods.
