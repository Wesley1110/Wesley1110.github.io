Teaching

Derivations with an economic reason.

My approach starts with the economic question, revisits the necessary mathematical foundations, and works through the derivation step by step. Where useful, Python simulations connect an analytical result to a numerical example that students can explore themselves.

These materials support courses in option valuation and advanced quantitative methods.
