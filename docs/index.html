Researching how information becomes price.

I am a PhD candidate in Finance at National Chengchi University. My research examines how information, uncertainty, and investor beliefs shape asset prices and investment decisions. I combine empirical analysis with theoretical modeling, with interests spanning asset pricing, macro-finance, behavioral finance, and artificial intelligence.

About

My empirical work studies policy uncertainty, central-bank communication, ambiguity, and exchange-rate predictability. I combine financial data with text and audio analysis to examine how investors interpret information and how that information enters market prices.

Alongside my empirical work, I develop theoretical models of investor beliefs, delegated portfolio management, and AI in financial markets. These projects examine how information-processing capabilities, preferences, and institutional constraints shape stock demand, portfolio choice, and equilibrium prices.

I am currently a research assistant on a National Science and Technology Council project led by Professor Mi-Hsiu Chiang. The project studies climate-change risk using machine learning, GPT-based news extraction, and dynamic hedging portfolios.

In 2027, I plan to visit the Department of Finance at BI Norwegian Business School as a visiting PhD student with Professor Paul Ehling.
